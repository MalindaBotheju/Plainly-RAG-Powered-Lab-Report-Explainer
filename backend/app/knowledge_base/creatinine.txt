TEST: Creatinine

What it measures: A waste product from normal muscle activity that your kidneys filter out of the blood.

Why it's tested: Used to estimate how well the kidneys are filtering waste from the blood (kidney function).

What a high result can mean: Elevated creatinine can mean the kidneys are not filtering as efficiently as usual. Causes range from temporary dehydration to longer-term kidney conditions, and it can also be affected by muscle mass and certain medications.

What a low result can mean: Low creatinine is less commonly a concern and can relate to lower muscle mass.

Typical next steps if abnormal: A doctor will usually look at this alongside other kidney markers (like eGFR) and hydration status, and may recommend a repeat test.
