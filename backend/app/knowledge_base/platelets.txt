TEST: Platelets

What it measures: The blood cell fragments responsible for clotting and stopping bleeding.

Why it's tested: Checks whether you have enough clotting capability, and can flag some bone marrow or immune conditions.

What a low result can mean: Low platelets (thrombocytopenia) can increase bruising or bleeding risk. Causes range from viral infections and certain medications to autoimmune conditions or bone marrow issues.

What a high result can mean: High platelets can occur with inflammation, iron deficiency, or after surgery/infection, and less commonly from a bone marrow condition.

Typical next steps if abnormal: Usually re-checked, alongside a review of recent illness or medications, before deciding if further workup is needed.
