TEST: Sodium and Potassium (Electrolytes)

What it measures: Two key minerals (electrolytes) that regulate fluid balance, nerve signals, and muscle/heart function.

Why it's tested: Imbalances can affect heart rhythm, muscle function, and overall fluid balance, and are checked as part of routine metabolic panels.

What an abnormal sodium result can mean: High or low sodium is often related to hydration status, kidney function, certain medications (like some blood pressure or water pills), or hormone imbalances.

What an abnormal potassium result can mean: Potassium levels that are too high or too low can affect heart rhythm and muscle function and, if significantly abnormal, are taken seriously and may need prompt attention.

Typical next steps if abnormal: A doctor will usually want a repeat test soon, especially for potassium, along with a review of medications and hydration.
